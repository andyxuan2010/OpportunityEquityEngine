# Opportunity Equity Engine

Opportunity Equity Engine is an AI-powered opportunity matching platform for high school students.

The initial concept focuses on helping Montreal and Quebec students discover and prioritize STEM programs, university-preparation opportunities, scholarships, research programs, and volunteering opportunities based on their interests, location, eligibility, and deadlines.
