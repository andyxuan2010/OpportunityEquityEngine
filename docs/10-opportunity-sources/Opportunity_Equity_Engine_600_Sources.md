# Opportunity Equity Engine — 600-Source Opportunity Catalog

**Compiled for:** Grade 11 / secondary-school opportunity discovery  
**Primary geography:** Montreal / Quebec → Canada-wide → selected international opportunities accessible or relevant to Canadian students  
**Compilation date:** 2026-09-09  
**Total records:** 600 (100 per category)

## How to use this file

This is a **source catalog for an Opportunity Equity Engine**, not a claim that all 600 applications are currently open.

Annual programs frequently close and reopen. A closed 2026 page can still be a valid recurring source for the 2027 cycle. Before exposing an opportunity to users, the app should re-check the official source for current eligibility, dates, fees, location, and application status.

### Verification tiers

- **A — Live-checked direct source:** the specific page or current-cycle source was checked during this research pass.
- **B — Official source / institutional hub:** official university, government, nonprofit, hospital, or organizer source. The exact annual event or application cycle must be re-checked.
- **C — Discovery source:** reputable directory, aggregator, or broad source. Do **not** treat it as authoritative until the system resolves and validates the official organizer page.

### Recommended ingestion fields

`category`, `opportunity_name`, `organization`, `geography`, `audience`, `source_url`, `verification_tier`, `reference_event`, `last_verified`, `deadline`, `status`, `cost`, `eligibility_rules`, `official_source_url`.

## Data-quality rules for the Opportunity Equity Engine

1. **Never infer eligibility from the title.** Parse and store explicit grade, age, residency, citizenship, language, academic, financial, and location constraints.
2. **Prefer the official organizer page over aggregators.** Discovery sites are useful for finding opportunities, but deadline and eligibility fields must be reconciled with the official source.
3. **Store cycle-specific dates separately from recurring program identity.** Example: `program = McGill Open House`; `cycle = 2026`; `event_date = 2026-10-24`.
4. **Do not label a program “open” unless an official current-cycle source confirms it.**
5. **Record costs and bursary availability separately.** A paid program with substantial need-based aid should not be treated the same as an unaided paid program.
6. **Distinguish direct Grade 11 eligibility from future-pathway references.** Some research sources in this catalog primarily serve CEGEP/undergraduate students and are included as pathway/reference sources, not immediate Grade 11 applications.
7. **For health, identity, immigration, disability, and financial-need eligibility, minimize sensitive data collection.** Prefer user-controlled filters and explicit consent.
8. **Add an automated `last_verified` timestamp and stale-data policy.** High-value records should be revalidated at least every 30–60 days during active application seasons.
9. **Preserve bilingual source text when relevant in Quebec.** Do not translate eligibility language in a way that changes its legal or administrative meaning.
10. **Rank by fit only after hard eligibility gates.** A 95% semantic match is irrelevant if a student fails a mandatory age, grade, residency, or citizenship rule.

## Important limitation

This is a **research seed dataset**, not a legally or administratively authoritative database. Program names, availability, eligibility rules, and URLs can change. Tier A entries received the strongest direct verification during this pass; Tier B and C records should be refreshed by the application before they are presented as current opportunities.
